# Store listing

**Name**: Capteur vidéo

**Summary (132 chars max)**:
Save the videos of the pages you visit as MP4, MP3 and more, from a simple side panel.

**Description**:

Capteur vidéo spots the videos playing in the active tab and lets you save them in one click.

• Clean side panel: detected videos show up while they play.
• Pick the format and quality: MP4, MP4 without sound, MP3 (128 to 320 kb/s), M4A, WAV.
• Separate video and audio tracks merged automatically (Instagram, Facebook…).
• HLS and DASH streams, quality picker.
• "Force detection" button when a video does not show up.
• Built-in diagnostic log to understand any failure.
• Everything happens in your browser: no data is collected or sent anywhere.

Limits: DRM-protected content (Netflix, Disney+, Spotify…) and YouTube are not supported.

Use Capteur vidéo for your own content, freely licensed content, or content you are allowed to copy for private use.
