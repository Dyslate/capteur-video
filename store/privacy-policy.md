# Privacy policy — Capteur vidéo

_Last updated: October 17, 2026_

Capteur vidéo does not collect, store on a server, sell or share any personal data.

**What the extension processes, locally only**

- The addresses of media requests made by the active tab, to list the videos you can save. This list is kept in the browser's session storage and erased when the tab is closed or the browser restarts.
- The media files you choose to save, downloaded directly from the site that serves them and written to your downloads folder.

**What it never does**

- No analytics, tracking, advertising or account.
- No data sent to the developer or to any third party.
- No access to your browsing history beyond the active tab's media requests.

**Optional local helper** (personal build only): it runs on your computer and talks to the extension through Chrome's native messaging. It downloads the page you ask for with yt-dlp; nothing is sent to the developer.

**Contact**: open an issue at https://github.com/Dyslate/capteur-video/issues
